Products are the cartons, pallets, or items being loaded. Containers are the trailers, ocean boxes, or pallets they go into. Dimensions are interior inches. Weight is pounds. Optimize shows the 3D load first. Issue plan is what assigns the plan number.

Containers

Choose a saved container and its interior, payload, and direction fill the highlighted row. Available is how many of that type can be used. Nose to door loads a trailer from the front wall toward the doors. Floor to top loads a pallet or carton upward.

Products

Products load upright first. If a piece does not fit, it can turn on its side or end when that orientation is allowed. A cylinder loads along its length, from the nose toward the door, unless its orientations say otherwise. Placement starts at the nose, the left wall, and the floor.
